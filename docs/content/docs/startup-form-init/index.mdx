## Adding a company

A new startup arrives as a deck and a thread. This gets it into the Venture base
without anyone re-typing the deck into the form — and without Claude ever
creating the record itself, because every new record emails the whole venture
team with the deck attached, and only you can attach the deck.

<Steps>
<Step n="01" title="Hand Claude the deck and the thread">

Attach the deck as a PDF, attach or paste the email thread, and say:

<Say>Add Marrowlight Bio to Airtable.</Say>

If there is no thread — you met the founder at an event — paste what you know
instead. Claude also asks once whether there is anything else you want in the
"How did this deal come to Portal" field, and the first time it runs, who is
filling out the form.

</Step>
<Step n="02" title="Claude checks it is not already there">

Before drafting anything, Claude searches the Startups table three ways: by
name with suffixes like Inc, Bio and Therapeutics stripped, by the website's
domain, and by each founder's name. You see every candidate with its pipeline
stage and the date it was added. A match stops the run with a link to the
existing record — and a pointer to the triage plugin, if what you actually want
is to update it.

</Step>
<Step n="03" title="Read the proposal">

One table, every row of the form, each value tagged with where it came from —
`Deck p.4`, `Email`, `Web: url` or `Inferred`. Rows Claude is leaving blank are
in the table too, with the reason. The "How did this deal come to Portal" text
is shown in full, because it is the record's Origin and it is the one thing
Claude composes.

Under the table: the two stage questions, which always need an explicit

and to whom. Nothing is written anywhere until you confirm.

</Step>
<Step n="04" title="Approve what needs creating">

The form's Team and Scientific Founder fields link to the HubSpot CRM table,
which is synced *from* HubSpot — so a founder who is not there has to be created
in HubSpot first. Claude does that through the HubSpot connector on its own
separate yes, matching the properties the Portal contact form sets, then waits
for the sync to bring the contact into Airtable. A missing parent org goes
through the new-organisation form, again on its own yes. A missing investor firm
stops the run: creating investors is out of scope for now.

</Step>
<Step n="05" title="Open the link, attach the deck, submit">

Claude hands you a pre-filled form link and three reminders: attach the deck to
field 8, check the two stage fields took, and fill in by hand anything on its
list of fields that could not be prefilled. You take a last look and click
Submit. That click is the only thing that creates the record.

</Step>
<Step n="06" title="Tell Claude it is submitted">

Claude finds the record created today under that name, reads back every field
it proposed, and reports anything that did not land — a select that dropped, a
link that did not take, something you changed by hand. Then it links the record
page.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What fires when the record is created

Claude shows this at the gate, filled in for the values you approved. None of it
depends on how the record is created — every automation listens for *record
created*, not for a form submission — so what matters is what is in the record
at that moment.

<Facts>
  <Fact title="New Company Form">Emails the core venture list and the fellows, plus a regional list chosen from Geography, CCing anyone in "Cc others" and you as submitter, with the deck attached. This is why the deck must be on the record at creation, and why you submit.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Cold Outreach Response">Stage 0.0 (outside core geographies) plus "Send BD Outreach?" = Yes emails the company. Claude defaults to No, and Yes needs its own yes.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Assigned notification email">Setting a Diligence Lead on an On Deck company emails them.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Investor tracker">If the introducing investor's diligence stage is set, an automation turns those fields into an investor note and clears them from the startup.</Fact>
</Facts>

## Good to know

<Facts>
  <Fact title="Claude never creates the record">Not through the API, not by submitting the form, not to test. Every new Startups record emails the whole team within five seconds. The submit is always your click.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Every value has a source">Each field is tagged with the deck page, the email, or the web page it came from. Nothing comes from Claude's own memory of the company, and a founder's email has to appear in the thread, the deck or a verifiable source — Claude never invents an address.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Duplicate check comes first">Name with suffixes stripped, website domain, founder names. A match stops the run before anything is drafted.</Fact>
  <Fact title="The stage questions always need an answer">Pipeline Stage and Membership Pipeline Stage are proposed but never assumed, because they decide which emails go out.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Missing people go into HubSpot, not the form">The Team fields link to a table synced from HubSpot, so Claude creates the contact there, on its own yes, and waits for the sync.</Fact>
  <Fact title="It cannot attach the deck">Same limit as the triage plugin: the Airtable connector has no file upload. You attach it in the form — which is the whole reason the human submits.</Fact>
</Facts>
