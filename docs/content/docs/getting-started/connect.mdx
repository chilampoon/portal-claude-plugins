---
title: Connect your accounts
description: Sign in to Otter.ai and Airtable with your own Portal accounts.
---

The first time you use a plugin, Claude asks you to sign in. Approve with your
own Portal accounts.

| Plugin | Needs |
| --- | --- |
| Investor Meeting Summary | Otter.ai **and** Airtable |
| Triage Note Updates | Airtable |

## Access is yours, not shared

Claude only ever sees what you can already see in those tools. Otter searches run
against your own account, and Airtable reads and writes as you.

One consequence worth knowing: if a colleague recorded the meeting, it is not in
your Otter. Ask them to share the conversation with you, or let them log it from
their own account.

## Reconnecting

Both plugins check their connectors before doing anything else. If a connector is
missing or a sign-in has expired, the plugin stops immediately — before reading
anything or writing anything — and tells you which one to reconnect.

In a chat, that is the **+** menu → **Connectors** → toggle it back on.
