## Adding a company

A new startup arrives as a deck and a thread. This gets it into the Venture base
without anyone re-typing the deck into the form — and without Claude ever
creating the record itself, because every new record emails the whole venture
team with the deck attached, and only you can attach the deck. Claude asks as
it goes: each step ends with a question or a confirmation, and nothing moves
until you answer.

<Steps>
<Step n="01" title="Name the company">

<Say>Add Marrowlight Bio to Airtable.</Say>

With Microsoft 365 connected, Claude searches your Outlook for the thread, reads
it, pulls the deck from it, and shows you what it found: the subject, who was on
it, the dates, the deck's filename. You confirm it is the right one. If it cannot
find them, or the deck is only a link, it asks you to attach the deck and paste
or attach the thread. No thread, because you met the founder at an event? Paste
what you know instead.

</Step>
<Step n="02" title="Claude checks it is not already there">

Before drafting anything, Claude searches the Startups table three ways: by
name with suffixes like Inc, Bio and Therapeutics stripped, by the website's
domain, and by each founder's name. A match stops the run with a link to the
existing record and a pointer to the triage plugin, if what you actually want is
to update it. A near-match is shown and you say whether it is the same company.

</Step>
<Step n="03" title="Answer its questions">

One short numbered message, before any drafting, with the default Claude would
propose beside each question so that "yes to all but 4" is a complete reply:

- who is filling out the form, the first time in a session;
- the deal source, and when it came through a VC, which investor and their
  diligence stage;
- the pipeline stage, which always needs an explicit answer because it decides
  which emails go out, with its follow-ups (a Diligence Lead for On Deck, the
  outreach email for companies outside the core geographies);
- the membership pipeline stage, which is yours to decide;
- anything to add about *how the deal reached Portal* that the thread does not
  show;
- anyone to Cc on the referral email.

Nothing is drafted until you reply.

</Step>
<Step n="04" title="Read the proposal">

One table, every row of the form, each value tagged with where it came from:
`Deck p.4`, `Email`, `Web: url` or `Inferred`. Rows Claude is leaving blank are
in the table too, with the reason. The "How did this deal come to Portal" text
is shown in full, because it is the record's Origin and the one thing Claude
composes: how the deal reached Portal, as fact from the thread, and nothing
about why we are looking.

<Step n="05" title="Approve what needs creating">

The form's Team and Scientific Founder fields link to the HubSpot CRM table,
which is synced *from* HubSpot, so a founder who is not there has to be created
in HubSpot first. Claude does that through the HubSpot connector on its own
separate yes, matching the properties the Portal contact form sets, then waits
for the sync to bring the contact into Airtable. A missing parent org goes
through the new-organisation form, again on its own yes. A missing investor firm
stops the run: creating investors is out of scope for now.

</Step>
<Step n="06" title="Open the link, finish the form, submit">

Claude hands you a pre-filled form link and four reminders: type the
Description, attach the deck, check the two stage fields took, and fill in by
hand anything on its list of fields that could not be prefilled. You take a last
look and click Submit. That click is the only thing that creates the record.

</Step>
<Step n="07" title="Tell Claude it is submitted">

Claude finds the record created today under that name, reads back every field
it proposed, and reports anything that did not land: a select that dropped, a
link that did not take, something you changed by hand. Then it links the record
page.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What fires when the record is created

Claude shows this at the gate, filled in for the values you approved. None of it
depends on how the record is created — every automation listens for *record
created*, not for a form submission — so what matters is what is in the record
at that moment.

<Facts>
  <Fact title="New Company Form">Emails the core venture list and the fellows, plus a regional list chosen from Geography, CCing anyone in "Cc others" and you as submitter, with the deck attached. This is why the deck must be on the record at creation, and why you submit.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Cold Outreach Response">Stage 0.0 (outside core geographies) plus "Send BD Outreach?" = Yes emails the company. Claude defaults to No, and Yes needs its own yes.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Assigned notification email">Setting a Diligence Lead on an On Deck company emails them.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Investor tracker">If the introducing investor's diligence stage is set, an automation turns those fields into an investor note and clears them from the startup.</Fact>
</Facts>

## Good to know

<Facts>
  <Fact title="Claude never creates the record">Not through the API, not by submitting the form, not through a browser. Every new Startups record emails the whole team within five seconds. The submit is always your click.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Outlook first, files second">With Microsoft 365 connected, Claude finds the thread and the deck itself and only reads; it never sends or moves mail. Without it, you hand them over.</Fact>
  <Fact title="It asks, then waits">Each step ends with a question or a confirmation. Nothing is drafted before your answers, and nothing is written before your yes.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Origin is the route, not the rationale">Who sent the company, to whom, when, through which channel, taken from the thread. Why we are looking is the venture team's to add later, and the triage plugin never touches this field either.</Fact>
  <Fact title="The description is yours">Claude leaves field 7 blank; you type it in the form before Submit.</Fact>
  <Fact title="It cannot attach the deck">Same limit as the triage plugin: the Airtable connector has no file upload. You attach it in the form, which is the whole reason the human submits.</Fact>
</Facts>
