## What it is

Every VC record in the Venture base has a **Group Overview**: who the firm is,
how it invests, where we stand, which of our companies it has seen, what is
open and what to watch out for, in six bullets and under 250 words. An Airtable
agent writes it from Portal's own meeting notes, the firm's stats and the
companies we have shared with it. No web research. Meeting notes outrank the
stats, and newer notes outrank older ones, so when a check size changes the
overview says the new figure and, if it matters, what it changed from.

It rewrites itself a couple of minutes after any note is logged on the firm, so
it is never older than the last thing anyone filed.

## Ask Claude for a brief

Before a call, say the firm's name.

<Say>Brief me on Redpine Ventures before my call.</Say>

Claude finds the record, confirming it when names are close, and gives you:

- **One line on whether the overview is current**, by checking its closing line
  against the notes on the record. If it is behind, or frozen by a hand edit, or
  empty, Claude says so rather than pretending.
- **The overview as written.**
- **The three newest notes**, one line each, with any status change they carried.
- The stats, the companies we have shared and the Portal contacts, if you ask.

It reads and never writes. If the overview is wrong, the fix is a new note, not
an edit.

## Three things to know

- **It is not yours to edit.** The agent never overwrites a cell a person has
  typed in, so one hand edit silently freezes that firm's overview for good. To
  correct it, log a note with the right information, through
  [Investor meeting summary](/docs/investor-meeting-summary) or the "New meeting
  or firm updates" form, and the overview rewrites itself.
- **It feeds the meeting note, not the other way round.** When Claude drafts a
  meeting note it reads the overview only to spot what changed since last time.
  Nothing from it is copied into the note, so a mistake in the overview cannot
  loop back into the record.
- **Personal details stay out.** Family, health and where someone lives never
  appear in it, even when a note mentions them. Professional background does.

*Switching on in October 2026. Until then the field is empty, and "brief me on"
briefs from the notes instead.*

## If you look after the base

<Say>Audit the group overviews.</Say>

Claude checks every VC with at least one real note and reports which overviews
are current, behind, missing their closing line, or empty, with the likely cause
and what to do in Airtable. It reports only; the fixes are a re-run of the agent
on a cell or a view. The setup of the agent itself, the prompt and the pilot
checklist are in the plugin's `docs/group-overview-field-agent.md`.

## Good to know

<Facts>
  <Fact title="Nothing is written by Claude">Both skills read. The only thing that writes Group Overview is the Airtable agent.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Only Portal's own data">Notes, stats and shared companies from the base. The agent's web search is off.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Newer wins">When two notes disagree, the later one sets the overview, and an older figure is mentioned only if the change itself matters.</Fact>
  <Fact title="When unsure, it asks">Similar firm names, and Claude shows the candidates rather than guessing.</Fact>
</Facts>
