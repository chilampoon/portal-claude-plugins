---
title: Log an investor meeting
description: After a VC meeting, say one sentence and Claude files the note from your Otter transcript.
---

Make sure the meeting is in your Otter, then run the plugin.

## 1. Say what happened

> I just met with Redpine Ventures — check Otter and update Airtable.

Add the date if the meeting was not today. If you want something extra in this
one note, ask in the same message — for example, *"...and include the technical
discussion this time."*

## 2. Read the draft

Claude shows you the note it wrote and the exact firm record it matched.

**Check the firm match.** The base holds genuinely different companies with near-identical names — WuXi Biologics,
WuXi AppTec and WuXi STA are three separate records whose CDAs do not cover each
other — and Otter mangles proper nouns, rendering one firm as "Wushi", "Wuji" and
"Wu Xi" in a single transcript.

Claude therefore prefers the **attendee's email domain** over the name it heard,
normalises trailing suffixes like Inc and Ventures before comparing, and breaks
ties on who is already linked to the firm. It deliberately ignores how many notes
a record already has, since that only reflects past use and would make a wrong
match reinforce itself. Confirm it anyway.

## 3. Say yes

Reply yes. Claude submits the CRM form, and the note appears on the firm's record
under your name and today's date.

## Things you can ask for in the same breath

None of these happen unless you ask:

- **A follow-up reminder** in 1–4 weeks. This sends a real email.
- **Notify a colleague**, by adding them to the notification list. Also email.
- **Credit the others who were on the call**, so they are recorded as Portal
  Contacts too. They have to be Airtable collaborators; anyone who is not gets
  named in the note text instead.
- **Flag someone who has left the firm.** Whenever it comes up in conversation,
  this is a free bit of CRM cleanup — they get hidden from the firm's people list.
- **Record a diligence status change** for a Portal company.
- **Update the firm's stats** — AUM, check sizes, whether they lead, fund stage,
  fund focus — when they actually told you.

## If you promised to send something

If the note records a commitment by you to send a company, a deck or a
non-confidential summary, Claude may offer to check whether it went out. It can
only do that if a mail connector is available in that chat.
