## After every meeting

Check the meeting is in your Otter, then say the line.

<Steps>
<Step n="01" title="Say what happened">

<Say>I just met with Redpine Ventures — check Otter and update Airtable.</Say>

Add the date if the meeting was not today. Want something extra in this one
note? Ask in the same message, for example *"…and include the technical
discussion this time."*

</Step>
<Step n="02" title="Read the draft">

Claude shows you the note it wrote and the exact firm record it matched.
**Check the firm match.** The base holds different companies with near-identical
names (WuXi Biologics, WuXi AppTec and WuXi STA are three separate records), and
Otter mangles proper nouns. Claude prefers the attendee's email domain over the
name it heard and ignores suffixes like Inc and Ventures when comparing, but
confirm it anyway.

</Step>
<Step n="03" title="Say yes">

Reply **yes**. Claude submits the CRM form, and the note appears on the firm's
record under your name and today's date.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Things you can ask for in the same breath

None of these happen unless you ask:

- **A follow-up reminder** in 1–4 weeks. This sends a real email.
- **Notify a colleague**, by adding them to the notification list. Also email.
- **Credit the others who were on the call**, so they are recorded as Portal
  Contacts too. They have to be Airtable collaborators; anyone who is not gets
  named in the note text instead.
- **Flag someone who has left the firm.** A free bit of CRM cleanup: they get
  hidden from the firm's people list.
- **Record a diligence status change** for a Portal company.
- **Update the firm's stats**, such as AUM, check sizes, whether they lead, fund
  stage and fund focus, when they actually told you.
- **Check whether you sent what you promised.** If the note records that you owe
  them a company, a deck or a non-confidential summary, Claude can check it went
  out, provided a mail connector is available in that chat.

## What lands in Airtable

One new note record, linked to the firm. Nothing that already exists is edited
or overwritten.

The twelve headings, in order: since last time, key highlights, fund and thesis,
companies they are working on, companies they are sharing with us, companies we
are sharing with them, passes and negatives, their process, co-investors they
named, people and personal, follow-ups, next contact. The three "companies"
headings stay separate on purpose: they are three different obligations.

## Good to know

<Facts>
  <Fact title="Nothing is written without your yes">Claude drafts, you approve. It never edits an existing note or creates a firm record.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Claude only sees your own Otter">Ask a colleague to share their recording, or let them log it themselves.</Fact>
  <Fact title="The note is about the investor, not us">Portal's own fundraising talk is left out, except what they committed to us and what we promised them.</Fact>
  <Fact title="It goes through the CRM form on purpose">Submitting the form is what tags you as Portal Contact on everyone you met.</Fact>
  <Fact title="When unsure, it asks">Similar firm names or no transcript that day, and it stops rather than guesses.</Fact>
</Facts>
