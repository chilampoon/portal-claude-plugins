## After every triage

Triage notes usually live in a Word doc or an email thread. This moves them onto
the company's record.

<Steps>
<Step n="01" title="Hand Claude the note">

Attach the Word doc or PDF, or paste the email in, and say:

<Say>Here are my triage notes on Marrowlight Bio — add them to Airtable.</Say>

Several people's notes on the same company at once is fine. Say so, and Claude
merges them while keeping each person's contribution attributable.

</Step>
<Step n="02" title="Read the draft">

Claude reorganises the note into Portal's six triage sections: Startup Overview,
Strengths, Weaknesses, Obstacles, Questions, Recommendation. It keeps your
substance and your ordering and tightens the wording; it does not add analysis
you did not write. A section you left empty stays empty.

It also shows you the Startup record it matched, with the company's current
pipeline stage. Confirm it.

</Step>
<Step n="03" title="Say yes">

Claude lists, field by field, exactly what it is about to write and whether each
one is a first write or an append. Only after you confirm does it write.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What lands in Airtable

The company's record in the Startups table, which is what the **Deals**
interface opens when you click the company in Deal Flow. Each triage section is
appended to one rich-text field. Text that is already there stays exactly as it
is; yours goes underneath, under a dated header.

## Good to know

<Facts>
  <Fact title="Nothing is written without your yes">You see every field it will touch, and whether each is a first write or an append.</Fact>
  <Fact title="It files notes, it does not write them">Hand it nothing and it asks for your note.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Nothing existing is replaced">It reads each field first and appends underneath. If it cannot read a field, it stops.</Fact>
  <Fact title="Two fields are off limits">Pipeline Stage, because changing it emails the venture team, and Investment Chain of Logic.</Fact>
  <Fact title="It never creates a startup record">Add the company through the new-startup form first.</Fact>
</Facts>
