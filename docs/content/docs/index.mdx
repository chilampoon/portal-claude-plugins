Two Claude plugins that take something you already said or wrote and file it in
the Venture Airtable base — after you have read it and said yes.

## The two plugins

**Investor meeting summary.** After a VC meeting, tell Claude one sentence. It
reads your Otter transcript, drafts a structured note about what you learned,
matches the firm, and files it on that firm's record.

**Triage note updates.** Hand Claude your triage note — a Word doc, a PDF, or an
email you paste in. It organizes the note into Portal's triage template and
appends it to that company's Deal Flow record.

## The shape is the same

You supply the source. Claude drafts. You read it. You say yes. Only then does
anything reach Airtable.

Neither plugin invents content, creates companies or firms that are not already
in the base, or overwrites what a colleague wrote. When either one is unsure —
which meeting, which firm, which company — it stops and asks instead of guessing.
